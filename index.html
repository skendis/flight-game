✈ טסים לפועלים סנטר

★ Flying to Poalim Center ★

בנק הפועלים עובר לבית החדש! אספו את העובדים וטוסו איתם לפועלים סנטר.

Pick up employees waiting under the green beacons in the villages (up to 6 per flight) and fly them through the red arrival gate at the new Poalim Center HQ. Follow the arrow.
Every 2 floating logos upgrade your plane to a bigger one with more seats: 6 → 10 → 16 → 24.

⛽ Watch your fuel! The engine burns fuel, faster at full throttle and in bigger planes. Fly through the glowing FUEL cans to refill the tank. When it runs dry the engine quits: dive to keep your speed and glide to the nearest can.
